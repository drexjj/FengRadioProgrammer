Write to the radio?

TX accepts an absolute frequency, a shift such as +0.6 or -5, or off for receive-only. Tones take 100.0, D023N or D023I; leave blank for none. Type a frequency into an empty slot to create a channel. Tick rows to edit or delete several at once (shift-click picks a range). Drag ⠿ or use ▲ ▼ to move a channel.

Baofeng Web-Based Programmer, created by W9JES. Source and issues on GitHub.

    
Clone protocols, memory layouts and settings come from CHIRP's baofeng_uv17Pro , uv5r, baofeng_wp970i and baofeng_dm32uv drivers, and every model is checked byte for byte against CHIRP. Files saved here open in CHIRP, and CHIRP images open here.

    
Radios without built-in Bluetooth can use a TIDRADIO BL-1 or BL-2 wireless programmer on the mic/speaker jack. Web Bluetooth works in Chrome, Edge and Opera on desktop and Android. On iPhone use the Bluefy browser. The page must be served over HTTPS or from localhost.
